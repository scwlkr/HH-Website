# Plan Your Home

Plan Your Home helps prospective new-home customers create a useful project brief by moving through an illustrated home. Refinement preserves that walkthrough while improving its visual quality and clarity.

## Language

**Project start**:
The public entry page that presents Plan Your Home as the primary path and a short general inquiry for other work as a subordinate fallback. Both paths remain available to every visitor; the project type provides guidance rather than access control.
_Avoid_: Intake register, eligibility gate

**Plan Your Home**:
The customer-facing guided planning experience for detached single-family new-home inquiries. It produces a project brief, while illustration supplies context and continuity without competing with the customer's current planning task.
_Avoid_: Tour, questionnaire, contact form

**Illustrated home**:
The coherent fictional house that connects all seven zones through recurring architectural and material cues. It provides continuity without representing a floor plan or design proposed for the customer.
_Avoid_: Model home, floor plan, customer design

**Project brief**:
The structured description of a prospective customer's new-home needs produced by Plan Your Home.
_Avoid_: Contact form, submission

**General project inquiry**:
The short, single-screen request embedded on Project start, primarily for work outside the detached single-family new-home path but available to every visitor. It captures minimal contact information and high-level project context without producing a detailed project brief.
_Avoid_: General project brief, detailed intake

**Zone**:
One of the seven fixed areas of the illustrated walkthrough that groups related planning prompts.
_Avoid_: Step, section, page

**Scene**:
A non-realistic architectural concept sketch that gives spatial context to a zone's prompts. Its composition is designed for a phone first and adapted to wider screens, using confident ink lines, construction marks, selective shadow, and translucent material washes without looking like a promised design.
_Avoid_: Background, rendering, room page

**Exterior elevation style**:
A single recognizable architectural direction represented by a straight-on front elevation with coherent form, roof, opening, porch, material, and detail cues. It records customer preference without depicting a promised customer design.
_Avoid_: Catalog, Transitional, combined-style label, promised design

**Prompt**:
One planning question presented in relation to a meaningful object or area in the illustrated home.
_Avoid_: Form field, survey item

**Prompt anchor**:
The meaningful object or area in a scene associated with the current prompt. It is highlighted automatically and never has to be discovered or clicked to continue.
_Avoid_: Hotspot, debug label

**Refinement loop**:
The lightweight change, test, capture, and review cycle used to improve Plan Your Home while preserving customer clarity, accessibility, and project-brief usefulness.
_Avoid_: Certification framework, visual approval system

**Review note**:
An owner's observation about friction encountered while using the website, paired with the desired outcome and a review target. It records the problem without prescribing implementation.
_Avoid_: Ticket, requirement, permanent backlog item

**Review target**:
A stable semantic address for the reviewed experience, such as route, viewport, zone, prompt, section, or visible control text.
_Avoid_: Pixel coordinate, DOM selector

**Development issue**:
A bounded, independently verifiable unit of work derived from one or more related review notes and tracked in GitHub.
_Avoid_: Comment, whiteboard, feedback register

**Approved direction**:
The Welcome and Living Room visual treatment explicitly accepted by the owner before that treatment is applied to the remaining zones.
_Avoid_: Automatic baseline, final design
