# Context Map

## Contexts

- [Plan Your Home](./CONTEXT.md) — defines the illustrated project-brief experience.
- [HHQ](./app/admin/CONTEXT.md) — defines the internal staff operations workspace.
- [Agent Guidance](./docs/agent-guidance/CONTEXT.md) — defines how internal and external agents receive consistent HH website context.

## Relationships

- **Agent Guidance → Plan Your Home**: Agent guidance points each audience to the appropriate Plan Your Home surface without redefining the product's language or rules.
- **Plan Your Home → HHQ**: Plan Your Home sends saved and submitted project briefs to HHQ for staff review and follow-up.
