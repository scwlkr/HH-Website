# H and H Advancement LLC (H and H / Howeth and Harp) Website

## purpose 

this website is for `h and h` a multi-disciplined company that does land development, architectural design, and building/construction/remodeling.

## naming

the business naming is a little confusing, but the colloquial name should always be `h and h` the full legal name is longer `H and H Advancement LLC` but that is really only used for legal papers, and not really public. `h and h` stands for `Howeth and Harp`

acceptable names: HH, H and H, Howeth and Harp
never use: H & H, never use the & ampersand symbol in the naming. It is extremly bad, never do this.

## git

- commit frequently, and verify branches are synced
- when using branches verify that you are using the right branch, and merge back to main as soon as possible. long lasting branches create clutter, and are not organzied easily
- every task ends with `git status`; remove task-created worktrees, numbered duplicate files, and generated artifacts before handoff, but never delete user-owned or unmerged work
